Project in one paragraph

ARBOR is a custom-built AI system for a solo/small tree service in Hampton Roads, VA. It starts as an AI receptionist that answers the phone under strict guardrails (never quotes prices, never diagnoses a tree, goes silent when you're on a real call) and grows into the nerve center of the whole business: it books into your color-coded Google Calendar with ZIP-cluster routing, files signed contracts and photos into Drive, runs 2-day estimate follow-ups with proof of insurance, detects when you're running behind and reschedules for you, and screens properties for Bay-protection permits before a cut. Its centerpiece is predictive property intelligence — a living record of every property you touch that forecasts recurring work and generates its own repeat business. It is handed to Claude Code as a staged, self-auditing build brief, with the goal of shipping as an app on your phone.

The through-line

  • You stay in control. Every idea that could commit you — a price, a booking, a permit filing, a firm date — is recommend-only. ARBOR proposes; you decide.
  • Data compounds. The system is built to collect every scrap of information per property so it gets smarter every year you run it.
  • Built in stages, audited every 5 steps so Claude Code can't wander down a rabbit hole.
